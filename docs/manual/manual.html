Task Reminder

A calm, Pokémon-themed to-do list that reminds you before things are due.

User guide · Android app & web

1Install the app

On Android (Android 7 or newer):

  1. Make sure Google Chrome is installed. The app runs on Chrome, even if another browser is your default. Without Chrome, reminders won't work.
  2. Open the APK file you were sent, or download the newest one here:
    github.com/Ansh-mick27/task-reminder-app/releases/latest/download/task-reminder.apk
  3. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source (your browser or file manager), then tap Install.
  4. Open Task Reminder from your home screen.

On any computer or phone browser, go to
https://task-reminder-f7a06.web.app

Updates: most updates arrive on their own the next time you open the app. You only need to install a new APK if you're told to.

2Sign in with Google

Tap Sign in with Google and choose your account.

  • Your tasks are saved to your Google account, so they're there every time you sign in, on any device.
  • Only you can see your tasks. Each person who signs in gets their own private list.
  • Your list also opens without internet; changes sync when you're back online.

3Your tasks at a glance

Every task is colored by how soon it's due. Red tasks always stay at the top, and colors update on their own as time passes.

RedUrgent: due in less than 3 days, or overdue.
YellowComing up: due in 3 to 7 days.
GreenLater: more than 7 days away.

Each card shows the time left (for example in 1 day or Overdue by 2 h) and the exact due date and time.

Squirtle, in the bottom-left corner, is your buddy. Tap it for a little encouragement.

Task list

4Add, edit and complete tasks

Add a task

Tap the Poké Ball + button (bottom right). Enter a title, the due date and time, and optional notes, then tap Save.

Edit or delete

Tap a task card to change it. To remove it, tap Delete in the same screen.

Mark as done

Tap the small ball on the left of a task. A Poké Ball catches it 🎉 and the task moves to the Done list at the bottom. Tap Done to show or hide it. To reopen a finished task, tap its ball again.

5Turn on reminders

The first time you open the app, tap Enable on the banner (or in Settings ⚙) and choose Allow. Do this once on each phone or computer where you want alerts.

🔴 Turns urgentwhen a task has less than 3 days left
🔥 Due tomorrow1 day before the due time
⏰ Due nowat the due time
☀️ Daily summaryevery morning, a list of your red tasks (8:00 AM by default)

Reminders are checked about every 10 minutes, so an alert can arrive a few minutes after the exact time.

Settings ⚙

  • Reminders: shows whether alerts are on for this device.
  • Daily summary: pick the time, or choose Off.
  • Sign out: stops alerts on this device.

6If something isn't working

No notifications arrive

  • Open Settings ⚙ in the app. It should say "On — you'll get alerts on this device". If not, tap Enable.
  • Check Android Settings → Apps → Task Reminder → Notifications is turned on.
  • Make sure Google Chrome is installed and up to date.
  • Battery savers can delay alerts. If they're late, allow Chrome to run in the background.

"Chrome is blocking notifications for this app"

Open Chrome, go to task-reminder-f7a06.web.app, tap the icon left of the address → Permissions → Notifications → Allow. Then close and reopen the app and tap Enable again.

Still stuck?

In Settings ⚙, tap Reminder details and send a screenshot of it to whoever shared the app with you. It shows exactly what's going wrong.
